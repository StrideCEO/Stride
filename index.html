Why Stride Exists

Stride was created by Johnathan Mendoza, a 20-year-old collegiate runner at Fullerton College, to redefine how runners train and connect.

After taking running seriously during his junior year of high school, Johnathan recognized a gap in the tools available to athletes—powerful data without intuitive guidance, and communities without meaningful structure.

Stride was built to close that gap by combining intuitive design with AI-powered training and coaching. The platform helps runners track progress, set meaningful goals, and train with data-driven confidence.

More than a training tool, Stride is designed to bring strong running communities together across every level of the sport.

Smarter training leads to better performance and long-term growth. Stride exists to empower runners everywhere to train smarter—together.

About Us.

Stride is built for runners who want clarity — not chaos. We combine training structure, personal records, and an AI Coach that helps you make better decisions day to day.

The goal is simple: help runners train with intention, stay consistent, and improve faster — without feeling lost.

Features

Dashboard that keeps you locked in.

A clean weekly view so you can see your progress fast — mileage, trends, and what matters without getting overwhelmed.

Training plan that stays simple.

Your week laid out clearly — so you always know what’s next, what’s completed, and how your plan is building over time.

AI Coach that actually helps.

Ask questions, get pacing advice, recovery tips, and training guidance — all inside your running ecosystem.

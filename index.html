Run Smarter with Stride

Stride is a modern running app designed to help runners train intelligently, track real progress, and build consistency with purpose.

Features

Personal Records

Automatically track PRs across distances and see real improvement.

AI Coach

Smarter insights to guide training decisions and recovery.

Training Plans

Structured plans built for growth, consistency, and race prep.

Stride GPS

Accurate GPS tracking with real‑time pace and distance.

Race Log

Track races, results, and performance history.

Running Journal

Log how runs feel physically and mentally.

Social Feed

Connect with runners and share progress.

Why I Built Stride

I’m a 20‑year‑old runner who wanted a better way to train — one focused on understanding your body, long‑term progress, and training smarter instead of guessing. Stride was built to change how runners approach improvement.

Inside a Stride Club

Everything you need to run a team.

Leaderboards

See your whole team, ranked.

Rank every athlete by weekly mileage, then filter by tier. One screen tells you who’s putting in the work and who needs a nudge.

Roster

Your roster, live.

Every athlete in one place, with live data as their runs come in. Tap any runner to see their training for the week — planned and completed runs at a glance — keep private coach notes on each athlete (injuries, goals, reminders), and manage the whole program from your phone.

Tiers

Varsity to JV, organized.

Sort athletes into tiers — varsity, JV, training groups, whatever fits your program — each with its own chat so the right runners get the right message.

Training calendar

Plan the week, schedule it, share it.

Set weekly goals and assign a workout to any tier, so the right runners get the right training. Put practices and meets on the calendar and your team RSVPs in a tap — you see exactly who’s coming and get a heads-up the moment an athlete reserves a spot. Then share or export any day, week, or month as a clean PDF.

Splits

Built for the track.

Log workouts and splits down to the 400m lap. This is running-specific, the way generic team apps never could be.

Club AI

Your private coaching co-pilot.

Ask Club AI who your most recent members are, how the team is growing, which athletes are at risk of falling off, or for programming ideas and a summary of this week’s training. Private to you.

Chat & announcements

Keep the whole team in the loop.

A team-wide main chat plus announcements when it matters — meet times, weather calls, race-day reminders — and one-on-one messaging with any runner, so nothing gets lost in a group text.

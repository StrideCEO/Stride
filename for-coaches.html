Individual running apps

Great for the solo runner — and blind to your team. No roster, no leaderboard, no way to see how 30 athletes are training.

Generic team apps

Built for scheduling and logistics. They don’t know what a PR is, can’t track mileage, and were never built for running.

Stride

Running-specific training, PRs, and races plus real team management — rosters, leaderboards, tiers, and a shared calendar. The first app built for running teams.

Inside a Stride Club

Everything you need to run a team.

Leaderboards

See your whole team, ranked.

Rank every athlete by weekly mileage, then filter by tier. One screen tells you who’s putting in the work and who needs a nudge.

Roster

Your roster, live.

Every athlete in one place, with live data as their runs come in. Add your team, set yourself as leader, and manage the whole program from your phone.

Tiers

Varsity to JV, organized.

Sort athletes into tiers — varsity, JV, training groups, whatever fits your program — each with its own chat so the right runners get the right message.

Training calendar

One calendar for the whole team.

Set weekly goals and lay out the training the whole team can see. Everyone shows up knowing what the week looks like.

Splits

Built for the track.

Log workouts and splits down to the 400m lap. This is running-specific, the way generic team apps never could be.

Club AI

Your private coaching co-pilot.

Ask Club AI who your most recent members are, how the team is growing, which athletes are at risk of falling off, or for programming ideas and a summary of this week’s training. Private to you.

Chat & announcements

Keep the whole team in the loop.

A team-wide chat plus announcements when it matters — meet times, weather calls, race-day reminders — so nothing gets lost in a group text.

No per-athlete fees

Free for your whole team.

Bring two runners or two hundred. Stride for teams is free — no per-seat pricing, no contracts. One coach sets it up, the whole roster runs on it.

Stride Run FAQ

Questions coaches ask about Stride

Stride Run is a free team app for cross country and track coaches. Here are straight answers to what coaches ask before bringing their team.

Frequently asked questions

Is Stride free for coaches and teams?

Yes. Stride is free to download and free for coaches and athletes: your club, team chat, announcements, polls, the team calendar, training groups, the weekly mileage leaderboard, up to 5 saved workouts, run tracking, PRs and race logs. Athletes never pay to be on a team.

Coach Premium ($19.99/month or $159.99/year) is optional. It adds attendance, daily readiness check-ins, weekly team goals, each athlete’s training at a glance, private coach notes and more leaderboard views. Every new club gets those free for its first 7 days.

How do I add my team?

Download Stride, open Clubs and tap “Start a club”. You only need a name, your city and one line about your team.

Then either hold up the QR code at practice (tap the QR icon at the top of Clubs, then “Show QR at practice”) or paste the ready-made invite message in your team’s group chat. Athletes download Stride and enter your team code when they sign up, and you see them join live. It takes about two minutes.

Can my athletes see practice times and workouts?

Yes. Practices, meets and workouts you put on the team calendar show up for the whole team or just the training group you choose, and announcements go to everyone. Athletes RSVP in a tap, and with notifications on they hear about it right away.

Does Stride work for cross country and track?

Yes, it’s built for both. You can log XC and track seasons and meets: results rank by time, and XC meets get top-five team scoring automatically. Athletes log track workouts with splits down to the 400m lap, and training groups let you split distance and sprints, or varsity and JV. It works for road running clubs too.

How is Stride different from Strava or TeamSnap?

Strava is built for individual athletes to record and share their activities; its clubs give you a shared feed, a weekly leaderboard and group events. TeamSnap is built for team logistics across many sports, like schedules, availability, messaging and payments, but it isn’t a running log.

Stride does both jobs for running teams in one app: practice times, announcements and the roster, plus mileage, workouts, PRs and meet results.

Is Stride on Android?

Not yet. Stride is iPhone-only for now (iOS 17 or later). If some of your athletes use Android, email business@runstride.org and we’ll let you know when that changes.

Does Stride work with Garmin, COROS or Apple Watch?

Yes, through Apple Health. Runs from an Apple Watch, or from a Garmin or COROS watch that syncs to Apple Health, show up in Stride when athletes open the app. Athletes can also record runs with their phone’s GPS.

Is my team’s information private?

Your club’s chat, calendar and announcements are only visible to its members. Athletes’ email addresses and phone numbers aren’t shown to other users, and your coach notes are only visible to you.

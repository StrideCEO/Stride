About Us

Stride is a running app, brand, and community built around one message: Run Smarter. Built by a runner, for runners. Not a corporation - a real runner building the app he wished existed.

Whether you are chasing consistency, a new PR, or your next race, Stride brings AI coaching, PRs, race logs, training plans, mileage, clubs, and community together in one place.

Delete Your Account

You can delete your Stride account and its data at any time, right in the app. If you can't get into the app, email us and we'll delete it for you.

Email a Deletion Request Back to Support

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Stride and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Account Settings.
  4. Scroll down and tap Delete Account, then confirm.
  5. Enter the code we email you and tap Confirm Delete.

Your account is deleted right away and you're signed out.

Or ask us by email

Email contact@runstride.org from the email address on your Stride account with the subject "Delete my Stride account." We'll confirm it's you and delete the account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile, sign-in, and profile photo
  • Your runs, routes, personal records, goals, and training plans
  • Your running journal, check-ins, and AI Coach chats
  • Your messages, notifications, and club memberships

What we keep, and for how long

  • Everything is removed within 30 days. Backups are overwritten on a regular schedule.
  • We only keep what the law requires or what we need to stop fraud or abuse, and only for as long as we need it.
  • App usage analytics aren't tied to your profile once it's deleted. Email us if you want those records removed too.
  • Purchase records stay with Google Play or the App Store. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription, so cancel it in the Play Store or App Store first.

Delete some data without deleting your account

You can delete individual runs, posts, messages, and other content in the app at any time. You can also email contact@runstride.org and tell us what you want removed. See our Privacy Policy for more.

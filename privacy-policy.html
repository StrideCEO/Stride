Stride Legal

Privacy Policy

What Stride collects when you use the Stride app and runstride.org, how we use it, who can see it, and the choices you have. We wrote it in plain English. If anything is unclear, email contact@runstride.org.

Effective November 5, 2026.

Back to Home View Terms of Service

The Short Version

  • We collect what we need to track your training, run clubs and teams, and power coaching features.
  • We don't sell your personal information, and there are no ads in Stride.
  • Other runners can see your profile and runs unless you make your runs private. Coaches of clubs you join can see your runs, daily check-ins, and attendance.
  • Stride is for people 13 and older. If you're under 18, a parent or guardian must agree to our Terms and this policy.
  • You can delete your account and its information at any time in the app.

Information You Give Us

  • Account: your name, email address, and password. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider and are never visible to us. If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple may give us a private relay email address instead of your real one.
  • Profile: your photo, age, whether you're a runner or a coach, running experience, goals, weekly mileage, injuries you choose to share, and coach details like team size and events.
  • Training: runs you record or add, training plans, races and personal records, shoe mileage, and journal entries.
  • Daily check-ins (when your club uses them): sleep quality, energy, soreness, stress, mood, how your run felt, nutrition, and any notes you add.
  • Messages and posts: direct messages, group and club chats, comments, likes, polls, club reviews, and photos you share.
  • Clubs and teams: clubs you create or join, calendars, goals, attendance, race logs, and season awards.
  • AI conversations: what you type to the AI Coach or to a club's AI assistant.
  • Support: anything you send us when you contact support or give feedback.

Information From Your Device

  • Location: your precise GPS location while you record a run, including in the background with your phone locked if you allow it. We use it to map your route and measure distance and pace. If you allow it, we also use your location to show clubs near you. We turn the start of a run into a city and state (for example, "Anaheim, California") to show on your run.
  • Photos: only the photos you choose to upload.
  • Notifications: a device token so we can send the notifications you've turned on.
  • Device and usage information: device model, operating system, app version, IP address, crash reports, and which screens and features you use.

Apple Health and Connected Watches

  • With your permission, we read workouts, distance, routes, heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep, and steps from Apple Health. This is how runs from an Apple Watch, Garmin, COROS, or other device get into Stride. With your permission, we may also save workouts to Apple Health.
  • We use Apple Health data only to provide Stride's fitness features to you, and to coaches of clubs you join as described below. We never use it for advertising, never sell it, and never share it with anyone for marketing.
  • You can turn off Stride's access at any time in your iPhone's Health settings.

Purchases

  • Subscriptions are paid through Apple. We get your subscription status from Apple through RevenueCat. We never see your card details.
  • If you donate on our website, Stripe processes the payment.

Our Website

When you visit runstride.org, we use PostHog to count page visits and taps on download links, including which ad or link brought you to the site. This uses cookies and browser storage and doesn't identify you by name.

How We Use Information

  • To run Stride: record and show your runs, build training plans, and show your progress, records, and badges.
  • To run the social, club, and team features you use.
  • To provide the AI coaching and club AI tools you ask for.
  • To send the notifications you've allowed, like reminders, recaps, messages, and club updates, plus occasional messages from the Stride team.
  • To keep Stride safe: review reports, prevent abuse, fraud, and spam, and enforce our Terms.
  • To understand how Stride is used, and to fix and improve it.
  • To handle subscriptions and support, and to meet our legal obligations.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use it for targeted advertising.

What Other People Can See

  • Your profile: your name, photo, and badges are visible to other Stride users.
  • Your runs: shared in the feed with the people who follow you and the clubs you're in, unless you mark a run private. Watch runs follow your sharing setting. A run post can show your route map, distance, pace, time, photos, and the city where you started. Route maps can show where you start and finish, like your home, so make a run private if you don't want that.
  • Clubs and teams: club leaders and coaches (owners, captains, and managers) can see members' runs, training, daily check-ins, attendance, race results, and activity in the club. Members can see the club chat, leaderboards, and results. Club reviews show your name.
  • Messages: seen by the people in the conversation.

AI Features

  • When you use the AI Coach, Stride sends the information needed to answer you, like your recent runs, training plan, goals, age, injuries you've shared, and your message, to our AI provider, DeepSeek, which generates the response.
  • When a coach uses a club's AI tools, the club information the coach includes (such as the roster, training, daily check-ins, attendance, and race results) is sent the same way.
  • DeepSeek may process this information on servers outside the United States, including in China.
  • AI answers can be wrong. They aren't medical advice.

Who We Share Information With

We share information with service providers that help us run Stride, and only so they can do that work for us:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in, file storage, and server functions.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, Apple Health, maps and location lookup, notifications, and App Store purchases.
  • Expo: delivering push notifications and app updates.
  • RevenueCat: managing subscriptions. It receives your account ID, email address, and name.
  • Stripe: website donations and payments.
  • PostHog: app and website analytics and crash information. It receives your account ID, email address, and profile details like whether you're a runner or a coach.
  • DeepSeek: AI features, as described above.
  • Mapbox: maps in the app.
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim): turning the start point of a run into a city name.
  • Beehiiv: our newsletter, if you subscribe.
  • YouTube (Google): the videos on our website. They use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode, and YouTube's own privacy policy applies when you play one.

We may also share information:

  • With the people you choose to share with: followers, clubs, and people you message.
  • With services you connect, like Garmin, COROS, or Strava, under their own terms.
  • When the law requires it, to respond to valid legal requests, or to protect the safety of anyone, including by reporting suspected child exploitation to the authorities.
  • If Stride is merged with or acquired by another company, in which case this policy keeps applying to your information.
  • With your permission.

Children and Teens

  • Stride isn't for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If we learn that we have, we delete the account and its information. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email contact@runstride.org and we'll delete it.
  • Teens from 13 to 17 may use Stride only if a parent or guardian agrees to our Terms and this policy. We don't sell teens' information or use it for ads.
  • A parent or guardian can email us to review or delete their teen's information.

Coaches, Schools, and Teams

  • Coaches who use Stride with their athletes are responsible for following their school, district, or league rules, including getting any parent permission those rules require.
  • We use team and student-athlete information only to provide Stride to that team. We don't sell it, use it for targeted ads, or use it to build profiles for any other purpose.
  • A school or organization can ask us to delete its team's information by emailing contact@runstride.org.

How Long We Keep Information

  • We keep your information while your account is open.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your profile and the information tied to it, like your runs, check-ins, and messages, within 30 days. We only keep what we must for legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons. Backups are overwritten on a regular schedule.
  • If you also want your analytics records deleted, email us and we'll remove them.

Security

We protect your information with encryption in transit, access controls, and other safeguards. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we'll notify you as the law requires.

Your Choices and Rights

  • Edit your profile any time in the app.
  • Make runs private, change how watch runs are shared, leave clubs, and block or report other users.
  • Turn off location, Apple Health, photo, or notification access in your phone's settings. Some features stop working without them.
  • Delete your account in Settings > Account Settings > Delete Account.
  • Ask for a copy of your information, a correction, or deletion by emailing contact@runstride.org. We'll confirm it's you and respond within 30 days.
  • We won't treat you differently for using any of these rights.
  • California: we don't sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • EU and UK: we use your information to provide the service you signed up for, with your consent (for location, Apple Health, and check-ins, which you can withdraw at any time), for our legitimate interests in keeping Stride safe and improving it, and to meet legal obligations. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.
  • Do Not Track: the app doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites. Our website doesn't respond to browser Do Not Track signals and uses PostHog only as described above.

Where Information Is Processed

Stride is based in the United States. Your information is processed in the United States and in other countries where our service providers operate, including China for AI features.

Changes to This Policy

If we make important changes, we'll tell you in the app and ask you to agree again before you keep using Stride. The date at the top shows when this policy last changed.

Contact Us

Questions or requests about your information: contact@runstride.org.

Privacy Policy

Welcome to Stride. This Privacy Policy explains how we may collect, use, and handle your information when you use our app.

Back to Home View Terms of Service

Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, and profile details provided during sign-up.
  • Running Data: Distance, pace, time, and training progress.
  • Device Information: Operating system, app usage, and crash logs to improve performance.
  • Optional Data: Any additional details provided in user profiles, such as motivational quotes or upcoming races.

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide personalized running insights and recommendations.
  • Track and display your training progress.
  • Enable social and community-based features, if applicable.
  • Improve app performance and user experience.
  • Send in-app notifications, if enabled in the Profile settings.

User Controls & Data Deletion

  • You may update or modify your profile information subject to app functionality.
  • You can adjust notification preferences in Profile > Preferences where available.
  • Account deletion requests can be submitted through Profile > Account Settings and will be processed according to our policies.
  • For data-related questions, you may contact support, though response times may vary.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be reflected in the Profile > Privacy Policy section.

Contact Us

For any questions or concerns about your data, please contact contact@runstride.org.

Stride vs Strava for coaches

Strava is the biggest app for recording and sharing runs, and plenty of your athletes probably use it. It’s built for individual athletes, not for running a team. Stride is built for the team. Here’s where each one fits, and why many runners use both.

Written by the team that makes Stride, so we’ve tried hard to be fair.

Where Strava is better

  • Recording and sharing with friends outside the team, segments, and finding routes.
  • Direct syncing with almost every watch, and Android support.

Where Stride is better for a coach

  • Practice times, announcements and workouts that reach the right training group.
  • A roster you can organize, plus attendance and private coach notes (Coach Premium).
  • PRs, meet results and XC team scoring kept for the whole team, season after season.

Can athletes use both?

Yes, and many do: Strava for their personal log and friends, Stride for the team. If an athlete’s watch syncs to Apple Health, their runs show up in Stride too, so nobody has to log twice.

Bottom line: Keep Strava if your athletes love it. Use Stride to actually run the team: schedule, messages, workouts, mileage and results in one place, free for everyone.

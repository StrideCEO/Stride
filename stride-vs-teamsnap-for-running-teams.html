Stride vs TeamSnap for running teams

TeamSnap is a popular, solid app for running the logistics of a team in almost any sport. Stride is built only for running teams: it does the logistics and the training. Here’s where each one fits, including where TeamSnap is the better pick.

Written by the team that makes Stride, so we’ve tried hard to be fair.

Choose TeamSnap if…

  • You manage teams in several sports from one account.
  • You need to collect fees, registration or payments through the app.
  • Some of your families are on Android and need an app today.

Choose Stride if…

  • You coach runners and want the training in the same place as the schedule: mileage, workouts, splits, PRs and meet results.
  • You want one place for practice times and announcements that athletes actually open, because it’s also where they log their runs.
  • You want it free for the whole team, with no per-athlete fees.

Bottom line: TeamSnap is the better general-purpose team app, especially for payments and Android. For a cross country or track team, Stride covers the logistics coaches use most and adds the running side TeamSnap doesn’t have.
